About our Sabbatical

Andrew got tenure at George Washington University in 2025, and was awarded a sabbatical semester for Spring 2026. Christina left her job at GWU in May 2025, worked as Interim Executive Director for a neighborhood organization called Greater Brookland Intergenerational Village, and that gig ended in December 2025.

We decided to capitalize on this unique opportunity and take our kids out of school to do an extended international trip.

Our approximate schedule was:

Connecticut Christmas- December 20th – December 26th

Visit Family in Kentucky – December 27th – January 2nd

Kenya – January 3rd through February 9th

Costa Rica – February 10th through March 31st

Guadeloupe – April 1st – May 9th

DC- May 9th- May 16th

Kentucky Farm – May 16th – June 13th

Connecticut family visits- June 13th- June 17th

Camp in New Hampshire – June 17th – August 22nd
